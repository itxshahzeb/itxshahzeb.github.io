summary

Integrations built the way Odoo expects them.

A courier that registers as a real delivery.carrier gets rate quoting, booking, tracking and cancellation for free, and keeps working when the client upgrades. A gateway on payment.provider inherits the whole payment lifecycle. That is the difference between an integration you maintain once and one you rewrite every version — and it is most of what I do.

The rest is domain depth: trade finance, distribution and eCommerce, where the hard part is not the API but the paperwork the business is legally held to.
